# Example history packs

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `demo.canon.json` | A small synthetic pack (a harbor town) used by the tests and the docs. |
| `frames-demo.canon.json` | A synthetic pack with time frames (another era with its own local time). |
| `unrelated-history.canon.json` | A second synthetic pack, for checks that must not mix two worlds. |
| `toaru-index.canon.json` | A full chronicle of *A Certain Magical Index* (Old Testament, New Testament, Genesis Testament and side stories): 1,873 events, 1,640 entities, 75 arcs, with natures and ways of thinking for the people who have a wiki page of their own. Built by `tools/pack-builder` from the sources below only. It is kept up to date here whenever it is rebuilt. |

## `toaru-index.canon.json`: sources and license

The pack is a derived work: its event summaries, entity descriptions, natures and ways of thinking were written by a language model from these wiki pages, and organized by code (`tools/pack-builder`, project `projects/toaru.json`):

- 灰机wiki — 魔法禁书目录中文维基 (<https://toaru.huijiwiki.com/>): arc pages, timeline pages (时间线) and the date research pages (时间线/考据), the pages of characters, organizations and places. Content licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
- The *A Certain Magical Index* Wiki on Fandom (<https://toarumajutsunoindex.fandom.com/>): arc pages and the chronology sections of character pages. Content licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

The pack is shared under **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0** (attribution to the wikis above, non-commercial, share alike). It contains no text of the novels, manga or anime themselves. *A Certain Magical Index* and its characters belong to their rights holders (Kazuma Kamachi, Kiyotaka Haimura, ASCII Media Works and others); this pack is an unofficial fan reference.
